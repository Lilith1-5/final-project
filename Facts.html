Facts

Violence against women has barely decreased in 20 years (only a 0.2% annual decline).
In OECD countries, violence against women costs about $110 billion per year.
Closing the digital gender gap could add $1.5 trillion to the global economy by 2030.
Full legal equality for women could still be 286 years away at the current rate of progress.
Women are more likely to have their pain underestimated or undertreated
Women can experience different medication effects because many drugs were historically tested mainly on men.
