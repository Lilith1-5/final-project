Home Safety

Stay safe at home.

▾
  • Lock doors and windows
  • Keep curtains closed at night to prevent people seeing inside
  • Keep a personal alarm or safety tool near your bed
  • Close the garage door immediately after entering
  • Never indicate you are alone when answering the door

Car Safety

Stay safe on the road.

▾
  • Lock doors immediately after entering
  • Park in well‑lit, busy areas
  • Check the back seat before getting in
  • Keep your phone charged and accessible
  • Trust your instincts

Date Safety

Stay safe when meeting someone new.

▾
  • Meet in public places for the first few dates
  • Tell a friend where you’re going and who you’re meeting
  • Keep your drink in sight at all times
  • Arrange your own transport
  • Set a check‑in message with someone you trust
  • Leave immediately if you feel pressured or uncomfortable

Night Safety

Stay alert after dark.

▾
  • Stick to well‑lit streets and busy areas
  • Avoid wearing headphones at full volume
  • Share your live location with someone you trust
  • Walk with confidence and purpose
  • Call for help early if you feel unsafe
