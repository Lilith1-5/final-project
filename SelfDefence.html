Self Defence Techniques

Basic defensive moves every woman should know.

Awareness First

The best defense is avoiding dangerous situations. Stay alert and trust your instincts.

React Quickly

If attacked. respond immediately. Surprise and speed are your advantages.

Target Vulnerable Areas

Eyes, nose, throat, groin, and knees are effective targets regardless of attacker's size.

Escape & Get Help

The goal is to create an opportunity to escape and get to safety. Do not try to win. Escape as quickly as it becomes possible.

Essential Defensive Techniques

Simple but effective moves to create distance and escape.

Palm Strike

Use the heel of your palm to strike the attacker's nose or chin, creating an opportunity to escape.

Nose, Chin

Elbow Strike

Drive your elbow into the attacker's face or torso.

Face, Jaw, Ribs

Groin Kick

Lift you knee and kick upward into the attacker's groin to cause immediate distruption and give yourself a chance to escape.

Groin

Eye Gouge

In a life-threatening situation, use your hand to disrupt the attacker's vision.

Eyes

Wrist Grab Escape

Pull your wrist sharply toward the attacker's thumb.

Wrist, Thumb

Forearm Shield

Raise your forearm to shield your head from the incoming strikes.

Forearm, Head Protection
