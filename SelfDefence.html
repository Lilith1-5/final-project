Self Defence Techniques

Basic defensive moves every woman should know.

Awareness First

The best defense is avoiding dangerous situations. Stay alert and trust your instincts.

React Quickly

If attacked. respond immediately. Surprise and speed are your advantages.

Target Vulnerable Areas

Eyes, nose, throat, groin, and knees are effective targets regardless of attacker's size.

Escape & Get Help

The goal is to create an opportunity to escape and get to safety. Do not try to win. Escape as quickly as it becomes possible.
